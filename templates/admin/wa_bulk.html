{{ ico('shield') }} Before you do this

Bulk messaging on WhatsApp is not email. People report it, and reports get numbers banned - including the one on your invoices and your website.

  • Message people who asked you something, not a bought list.
  • Say who you are in the first line. A template with {{ '{{ brand }}' }} in it does that for you.
  • Honour an opt-out the moment you get one - the list is checked on every send, bulk or single.
  • Keep runs small. {{ limit }} at a time is the default here for that reason.

The daily cap and throttle in Settings › WhatsApp apply to Cloud API runs.
